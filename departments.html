Macon County Departments

Explore the various departments that serve our community.

Health & Human Services

The Health & Human Services department focuses on community wellness, offering programs like healthcare, nutrition services, and child care resources.

Public Safety

Our Public Safety department is committed to keeping Macon County safe through emergency services, law enforcement, fire departments, and disaster response teams.

Public Works

Responsible for maintaining roads, bridges, and utilities, the Public Works department ensures the smooth operation of our county infrastructure.

Recreation

The Recreation department oversees parks, sports leagues, and community programs designed to enhance the quality of life for residents.
